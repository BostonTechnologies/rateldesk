## Historical effort and compatibility

New timeline events persist `Hours` and `WorkLogId`. API/MCP/CLI results preserve
numeric `hours` and add nullable `recordedHours` and `workLogId`. A recorded zero
is known zero; null is unavailable. UI refresh uses the additive measurement.
Pre-upgrade events have independent IDs and no direct worklog association.
A retained transactional outbox publication can recover a Worklog/InternalNote
effort snapshot only through exact event/ticket/type identity and valid,
unambiguous finite numeric evidence. Timeline effect kind, ordinal key
`0000:Timeline` through `0255:Timeline`, and a succeeded inbound receipt with
matching ticket/organization establish provenance. Missing, nonnumeric,
duplicate or nonfinite effort on an identifiable eligible candidate blocks
recovery even alongside a valid candidate. All candidates must agree; recovery
cannot establish the canonical WorkLogId. Missing,
malformed, conflicting or otherwise ambiguous evidence leaves snapshots null.
Repeated text, authors and nearby timestamps never drive a join. Canonical WorkLogs and totals remain unchanged.
The worklog itself remains authoritative where it can independently be read.
The signed public timeline builds entries from visible canonical WorkLogs, so
its existing worklog ID supplies authoritative historical effort directly; this
does not recover or guess the association of a separate legacy staff event.

New-column migrations preserve populated SQLite/PostgreSQL databases and their
history. Recovery scans retained outbox evidence once during migration. No per-event
read enrichment, outbox/history rewriting or historical worklog rewriting is
required. Live/read projections retain internal-note confidentiality, event
identity/order/count and signed inline-image behavior.
