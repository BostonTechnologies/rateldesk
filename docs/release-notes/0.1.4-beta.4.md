# RatelDesk 0.1.4-beta.4

- Search organizations and customer contacts from the same keyboard-accessible controls across ticketing and administration. AI Chat puts organization scope beside the workspace tabs. [#445](https://github.com/BostonTechnologies/RatelDesk/issues/445) · [PR #461](https://github.com/BostonTechnologies/RatelDesk/pull/461)
- Chat, Investigations and ticket assistants show tool starts, arguments and correlated outcomes. Expand a child run to inspect its history. A confirmed foreground completion can accept a new reply while its scheduled report is pending. [#443](https://github.com/BostonTechnologies/RatelDesk/issues/443) · [#446](https://github.com/BostonTechnologies/RatelDesk/issues/446) · [#447](https://github.com/BostonTechnologies/RatelDesk/issues/447) · [PR #457](https://github.com/BostonTechnologies/RatelDesk/pull/457)
- Portrait AI workspaces switch between a searchable full-width list and the selected conversation. Ticket assistants also support the reduced-height composer and fullscreen controls. [#448](https://github.com/BostonTechnologies/RatelDesk/issues/448) · [PR #458](https://github.com/BostonTechnologies/RatelDesk/pull/458)
- Request operators can close abandoned work with a reason. The history retains the provider outcome and attempt identifiers. Closing a parent also settles unfinished tasks. [#449](https://github.com/BostonTechnologies/RatelDesk/issues/449) · [PR #460](https://github.com/BostonTechnologies/RatelDesk/pull/460)
- Active human instance administrators with a verified ticket identity can be selected across organizations for incidents, requests, tasks and changes. [#452](https://github.com/BostonTechnologies/RatelDesk/issues/452) · [PR #459](https://github.com/BostonTechnologies/RatelDesk/pull/459)
- AI Ops, People & Organizations, Email, Integrations and Automation each have one navigation entry and tabs for the areas the operator can access. Existing deep links remain available. [#450](https://github.com/BostonTechnologies/RatelDesk/issues/450) · [#451](https://github.com/BostonTechnologies/RatelDesk/issues/451) · [#453](https://github.com/BostonTechnologies/RatelDesk/issues/453) · [#454](https://github.com/BostonTechnologies/RatelDesk/issues/454) · [#455](https://github.com/BostonTechnologies/RatelDesk/issues/455) · [PR #462](https://github.com/BostonTechnologies/RatelDesk/pull/462)
